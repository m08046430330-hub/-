### ストーリー（Story）
420 Weed Love Shop started with a simple idea: bring the beauty of Japanese calligraphy to the relaxed spirit of 420.

Every design is created in Japan. You'll find brush-style kanji like 大麻愛 (taima-ai, "cannabis love"), red hanko seals reading 四二零 / 420, and playful art like our Stoner Cats.

Our products are made to order with a trusted print partner, so nothing is overproduced and each piece is printed just for you. Production and shipping times are shown on every listing.

Questions or ideas for a design? Send me a message. I'm happy to help.
