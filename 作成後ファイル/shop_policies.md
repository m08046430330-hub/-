# Etsy ショップポリシー（英語・貼り付け用）

設定場所: ショップ管理ツール → 設定 → ポリシー設定（返品・交換ポリシー）／ショップについて → よくある質問（FAQ）

## 返品・交換ポリシーの選び方（Etsy の選択式）
- 返品: 受け付けない ／ 交換: 受け付けない
- 補足欄（下の英文）で「不良品・破損・間違いは無料で交換または返金」と明記する

## 返品・交換の補足（Returns & exchanges）
Every item is made to order just for you, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or the wrong size. Please check the size chart in the listing photos before ordering.

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted or defective, or you received the wrong item, we'll make it right. Message us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem, and we'll send a free replacement or a full refund. You don't need to send the item back.

## キャンセル（Cancellations）
Production starts soon after you order. If you need to cancel or change your order (size, color or address), please message us within 1 hour of purchase. After production has started, we can't cancel or change the order.

## 発送（Shipping）
Each item is printed and shipped by our print partner. Production usually takes 2-5 business days, and delivery times depend on your location (shown at checkout). You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

Please make sure your shipping address is correct. We can't be responsible for orders sent to an address that was entered incorrectly.

Customs and import duties, if any, are the buyer's responsibility.

## よくある質問（FAQ）
**Do you sell cannabis or smoking accessories?**
No. We sell original art printed on apparel and homeware only. No cannabis products or smoking accessories are sold here.

**What size should I choose?**
Our tees are unisex Bella+Canvas 3001 with a retail fit. Please check the size chart in the listing photos. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.

**How should I care for my item?**
Tees: machine wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low. Do not iron directly on the print. Mugs: dishwasher and microwave safe.

**Can I get a custom design?**
Send us a message with your idea and we'll let you know what's possible.
